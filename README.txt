# 📊 Amazon Products Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze **Year-to-Date (YTD) sales performance** of Amazon products.  
This project helps uncover **sales trends, top-performing products, category insights, and customer engagement metrics** to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard visualizes Amazon product sales data using **Power BI**, enabling stakeholders to gain insights into sales growth, customer preferences, and product performance. It provides interactive exploration through slicers and filters.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze **YTD and QTD sales performance**
- Identify **monthly and weekly sales trends**
- Highlight **top-performing products and categories**
- Measure **customer engagement using reviews**
- Enable **interactive data exploration**

---

## 📌 Key Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **YTD Sales** | $2.18M |
| **QTD Sales** | $811.09K |
| **YTD Products Sold** | 28K |
| **YTD Reviews** | 19M |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visual Components

### 🔹 Sales by Month (Line Chart)
Shows monthly trend to understand seasonal growth and overall revenue movement.

### 🔹 Sales by Week (Bar Chart)
Displays weekly performance to spot short-term spikes or dips.

### 🔹 Sales by Product Category
Breaks down sales across product categories to identify areas of strength.

### 🔹 Top 5 Products by YTD Sales
Highlights the highest revenue-generating products.

### 🔹 Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews
Shows products with the most customer engagement.

---

## 🎛 Filters & Interactivity
